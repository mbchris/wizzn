# wizzn

Wizzn is a mobile-first application for iOS and Android. It targets to make use of the time used for doom scrolling in social media apps by providing the user time to upskill, learn and intelligently entertain the user.

Focus are users who are addicted to the dopamin effect that apps likes instagram and tiktok provide. But endless doom-scrolling of irrelevant context shall be replaced by actual meaningful content.

The self-generated content can be either:
- audio content: 3-5 minutes of inspiration, completed per lesson. 1 to 3 target pictures / drawings to emphasize
- written content: 3-5 slider pages of a particular topic with linkout to the actul source. the slider pages complete the small aspect of the topic.

Content is fully AI pre-generated. For the content administrators, there is a way to add content and context for which the AI created the appropriate content.

There is a rewarding system for the user to spend time in the app and to finish at least 5 units per week. A streak system inspired by other apps shall be used.

The UX is modern, friendly and kind of playful. 

Target audience is 16+ in age.

Beginning a journey means to select fields of interests and ways to present. The available fields of interest are mainly out of everyday's needs, not too specific.

Starting with content about
- healthy nutrition 
  Themes: protein / vegan / vegetarian / keto / ...

- partnership
  Themes: seduction, needs, love, partnership balance

- finance
  Themes: investments, tax optimization, budget self-control

- sports
  Themes: establish a sports routine, types of workouts, strength, cardiologic sports

- psychology
  Themes: behaviour patterns, neurodivergence, Developmental Psychology, social psychology
